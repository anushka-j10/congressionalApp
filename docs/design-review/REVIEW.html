Options and tradeoffs

  • A — Civic form: blue actions and squared controls on white. Conventional and clear; deliberately uses no official seals or government branding.
  • B — Compact utility: neutral ink, compact header, direct upload controls and fact rows. Least decorative and best aligned with a short phone task.
  • C — Document reader: warm paper, serif headings and thin rules. More personal and reading-oriented, with a slightly less compact heading treatment.

All three include upload and illustrative results views. The results are newly fabricated layout examples, not held-out results or a model run. No API key, notice upload, voice output or model call is used by the previews. The actual review draft now uses the selected C style. A/B and the earlier C layout are preserved for comparison; this is not a shipped theme picker.

C refinement: reduce repetition and scrolling

The user selected C and asked for fewer repeated headings and less scrolling. NN/G’s research on AI prototyping describes generic and interchangeable results when task context is weak. That supports a task-specific review; it does not establish that any visual style proves AI authorship. NN/G’s progressive-disclosure guidance supports showing common tasks first and revealing secondary detail on request. Combined with GOV.UK’s direct-heading guidance, these informed the following design decisions:

  • Remove step kickers and the visible duplicate photo heading; keep one opening task heading and two photo actions.
  • Move photo tips, the unchanged original OCR and processing/privacy detail into named disclosures. Editable text and required confirmations remain directly available in the open review.
  • Fold the completed reading review after successful extraction. A failed extraction leaves it open for correction.
  • Show the verification definition once above the fields. Put deadline, amount and action first; use compact rows rather than repeating explanatory paragraphs inside every card.
  • Keep unverified-field warnings visible. Source snippets expand per field; Show source opens and highlights the full original text.
  • Fold the longer explanation while keeping its cautions, translation-fallback message and audio controls outside the disclosure.

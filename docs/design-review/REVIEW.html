What reads as a generated template here

These patterns do not prove a site was made by AI. The concern is their cumulative effect on this particular task.

Gradients, animated sparkle icons, fake conversational greetings and invented trust metrics are other commonly discussed template conventions, but they were not present here; the draft does not claim to have removed them.

Options and tradeoffs

  • A — Civic form: blue actions and squared controls on white. Conventional and clear; deliberately uses no official seals or government branding.
  • B — Compact utility (recommended): neutral ink, compact header, direct upload controls and fact rows. Least decorative and best aligned with a short phone task.
  • C — Document reader: warm paper, serif headings and thin rules. More personal and reading-oriented, with a slightly less compact heading treatment.

All three include upload and illustrative results views. The results are newly fabricated layout examples, not held-out results or a model run. No API key, notice upload, voice output or model call is used by the previews. The actual draft defaults to B; A/C are stylesheet alternatives for review, not a shipped theme picker.

Scope and repository

The current deployed code is already pushed to fix/notice-reliability. Formatting work is isolated on design/mobile-format-review. After review, the selected format can be merged/pushed to the deployment branch and deliberately redeployed. Render automatic deploys are off.

Verification states, source controls, field confirmation, amount attestation, translations and original-text access stay in the existing workflow. New Spanish/Tagalog interface strings remain unreviewed translations. No backend extraction prompt, fact validator, key or original held-out artifact is changed. Controls target at least 44px; phone-size preview measurements and regression checks are recorded separately after inspection.
